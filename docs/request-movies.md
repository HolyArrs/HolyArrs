# Adding and asking for movies

This page covers how movies get into HolyArrs: adding them yourself, asking for them on the Requests page, and letting Outside lists add them for you.

## Add a movie

1. Open **Search** and type a movie name, or paste `tmdb:` or `imdb:` with its id.
2. Press **Add** on the movie you want.
3. Pick the **Library folder**, the **Pick rules** and the **Release gate**. HolyArrs remembers your last choices.
4. Leave **Search now** on to start looking at once.

Want an HD copy and a 4K copy? Tick **Also keep a second Version**. The 4K Version gets its own Pick rules and its own folder, named with `[4K]`. You can add or remove Versions later on the Movie page.

## Why a movie is not searched yet

The Movie page has a **Why** line. It says when the Release gate opens, for example "closed until 2027-03-18 (90 days after the cinema date)". The **Dates** card lists the cinema, digital and disc dates.

## The Library

The Library screen filters by **Missing**, **Not out yet**, **Better copy wanted** and **In library**, and finds movies by name. It remembers posters or list view, and the filter you last used, in your browser.

## Requests

People who are members ask for movies; the owner and admins decide.

**Asking (members).** Search for the movie and press **Ask**. If someone already asked, press **Ask too**. The movie shows on your requests as **Waiting for approval**. You can take it back while it waits.

**Weekly limits.** Each person has three limits over a rolling 7 days: one for movies, one for shows and one for music. They are separate, so asking for a show never uses a movie ask. The defaults are 10 each, set in **Settings, Requests** (0 is no limit). The owner or an admin can give one person their own limits per kind on the **Users** page (**Change**, then **Weekly asks**; an empty box uses the default). People who may approve have no limit. Search and Requests say what is left, for example "You have 3 movie asks left this week". Over a limit, HolyArrs says which one and how long to wait, for example "try again in 6 days and 23 hours". Turned down asks do not count, and joining someone else's ask is free.

**Deciding (owner and admins).** Requests menu shows a badge with the number waiting. Press **Approve**, pick a Library folder and Pick rules (or keep the Request defaults), add a note if you like, and press **Approve and add**. The movie is added and HolyArrs starts looking for it. **Turn down** asks for a short reason, which the person sees.

**Being told.** Messages on the Requests page tell you when your ask is approved or turned down, and when the movie is ready to watch. Alerts (Settings, Alerts) can also send these to Discord or email.

**Who asked.** The Movie page has a Requests card naming who asked and who approved. Home shows the same in its latest events.

The default weekly limits, default folder and Pick rules, and search when approved live in **Settings, Requests**.

## Outside lists

Settings, **Outside lists** reads a TMDB or Trakt list on a schedule and adds its movies, each with its own folder, Pick rules and Release gate. Trakt lists need your Trakt key first. Movies on the **Never-add list** are always skipped. Press **Read now** to read a list at once.
