## Start here

| Step | Page | You end with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Install](start/install/) | HolyArrs open on port `6262` |
| 2 | [Folders](start/folders/) | One folder layout that moves files instantly |
| 3 | [First run](start/first-run/) | An owner account and your Library folders |
| 4 | [Connect](start/connect/) | A downloader and a source that both pass **Test** |
| 5 | [First title](start/first-title/) | One movie found, downloaded and in your Library |

Then see [Home and Settings](start/home-and-settings/) to find your way around.
