Decide on requests as the owner or an admin.

## Approve a request

1. Open **Requests**. The menu badge shows how many wait.
2. Open a request. You see what was asked and who asked.
3. The **Quality** row shows the copy they asked for. Pick another line to approve that copy instead. Folders and Pick rules come from the [Request rules](settings.md#where-approved-titles-go).
4. For a show or artist, untick seasons or albums you do not want.
5. Add a note if you want, and press **Approve**.

You should see the title in the Library. With **Search when approved** on, movies are searched at once and shows at the next feed check.

To clear a long list, press **Approve all**.

## Decline a request

1. Open the request and press **Decline**.
2. Write a reason, or pick a quick reason. The people who asked see it.

You can undo for a few seconds after you decline.

## Choices

### What approving changes

| Asked for | Approving does |
|---|---|
| A show already in the Library | Turns on the asked seasons |
| Music | Keeps only the asked albums up to date |
| An album kind the artist normally leaves out, such as a single or live album | Turns that kind on for the artist |

### Who asked

The movie, show or artist page has a Requests card: who asked, for which seasons or albums, and who approved.

## If it does not work

**Symptom:** "Tick at least one to approve."
**Cause:** every season or album is unticked. **Fix:** tick one, or decline.

**Symptom:** the approved title is not searched.
**Cause:** **Search when approved** is off, or a show waits for the next feed check. **Fix:** turn it on in [Request rules](settings.md#where-approved-titles-go).
