See a movie series together, or remove a whole series at once. HolyArrs groups movies by the series their movie details list, never by names or folders.

## See a series

1. Open **Library** and pick **Movies**.
2. Group by series.

You should see each series with its movies under it.

## Remove movies from a series

:::caution
Deleted files move to the holding folder and are removed for good after the holding days (7 by default).
:::

1. Open the series.
2. Pick the movies to remove and choose whether to delete their files.
3. Press **Review removal**. It lists every movie and file that will be touched.
4. Press **Confirm this exact selection**.

You should see each movie marked removed, skipped or failed.

Files stay by default. Deleted files HolyArrs imported or adopted move to the holding folder, as when [deleting one movie](organise-movies.md#delete-a-movie). Files HolyArrs does not own are never touched, and the series folder itself is never deleted.

## Choices

- **Something changed after the review**, such as a new file or a protected copy: HolyArrs refuses and asks you to review again.
- **A movie with a protected copy** is skipped, unless you also delete files and confirm the protected copy.
- **A failure** does not undo the others. Press **Review remaining movies** to try the rest again.
- **Outside lists** may add a removed movie again later, unless you add it to the Never-add list.
