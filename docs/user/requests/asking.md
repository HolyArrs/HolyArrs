# Ask for a title

You want a movie, a show or some music that is not in the Library yet. You ask for it, and the owner or an admin decides.

## Ask for a movie

1. Open **Search**, or press **Ask for something** on the Requests page.
2. Find the movie.
3. Press **Ask**.

The movie shows on the Requests page as **Waiting for approval**.

## Ask for a TV show

1. Find the show in **Search**.
2. Press **Ask**.
3. Pick the whole show, or tick only the seasons you want. The whole show means every season except specials, plus seasons that come out later.

If the show is already in the Library, you can only tick seasons it does not keep yet.

## Ask for music

1. Open **Search** and switch to **Music**.
2. Find the artist and press **Ask**.
3. Pick all albums, or tick only the albums you want.

Albums already in the Library cannot be ticked. If the artist is already in the Library, its page lets you ask for albums it does not keep yet.

## When someone already asked

If another person asked for the same thing, you join their request. Joining is free and does not count toward your weekly limit. You get the same news they do. If their request is still waiting and you want more seasons or albums, it grows to include yours.

## Take it back

While a request waits, open it on the Requests page and press **Take back**. It leaves Requests. You can ask again later.

## What happens next

Each request moves through these steps on the Requests page:

- **Waiting for approval**: nobody has decided yet.
- **Approved**, then **Searching**, **Downloading** and **Importing** while HolyArrs gets it.
- **Ready to watch**, or ready to listen to: the files are in the Library. For a show, every aired episode of the seasons you asked for is in. For music, every track of every album you asked for is in.
- **Declined**: you see the reason the approver wrote.

You get a message on the Requests page when your request is approved, declined or ready. If the owner set up alerts, you can get the same by Discord or email.

## Troubleshooting

**"Your account can't ask for titles yet."** The owner has not given your account the right to ask. Check with them.

**"You have used your 10 movie requests this week."** You hit your weekly limit for that kind. The message says how long to wait. Movies, shows and music each have their own limit, so you can still ask for the other kinds. See [Weekly limits](settings.md#weekly-limits).

**A season or album cannot be ticked.** It is already in the Library.
