# Requests

Requests let the people on your server ask for movies, TV shows and music. The owner and admins approve or decline each one. Approved titles go into the Library, and HolyArrs starts looking for them.

Open **Requests** in the menu, for example `http://your-server:6262/requests`.

## Pages in this section

- [Ask for a title](asking.md): for members who want something added.
- [Approve or decline](approving.md): for the owner and admins.
- [Weekly limits and Request rules](settings.md): the settings at `/settings/requests`.
- [Report a problem](reports.md): tell the owner a file is broken, at `/requests/reports`.

## Who can do what

- **Members** ask, take back an ask while it waits, and see their own requests and reports.
- **The owner and admins** see every request, approve or decline, answer reports and change the Request rules.
- People who may approve have no weekly limit.

## Held back

Two ways of creating requests on their own are held back from this release and do not show in the app:

- **Plex watchlists**: titles on a person's Plex watchlist becoming requests.
- **Trakt lists**: reading Trakt lists and adding their titles.
