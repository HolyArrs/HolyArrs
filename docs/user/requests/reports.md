# Report a problem

A file in the Library has a problem. The picture is bad, the sound drifts, or it will not play. You report it, and the owner or an admin fixes it.

Reports live at **Requests, Reports**, at `http://your-server:6262/requests/reports`.

## Send a report

1. Open the movie, show or album.
2. Choose **More**, then **Report a problem**.
3. Pick what is wrong: **Picture**, **Sound**, **Subtitles**, **Won't play** or **Other**.
4. Add a note if it helps, for example "the sound drifts after about 20 minutes".
5. Send it.

You can report a whole title, one season, one episode, one album or one track.

## See your reports

Open **Requests, Reports**. Members see the reports they made. The owner and admins see every report.

## Answer a report (owner and admins)

1. Open **Requests, Reports**.
2. Open a report and fix the file, for example by swapping it for a better copy.
3. Press **Mark fixed** and add a note. The person who reported sees it.

Fixed reports stay in the list. Press **Reopen** if the problem comes back.

## Troubleshooting

**There is no Report a problem option.** Reports are for titles in the Library. Open the title page itself.
