# Approve or decline

Someone asked for a title and you want to decide. This page is for the owner and admins.

## Approve a request

1. Open **Requests**. The menu shows a badge with how many requests wait.
2. Open a request. You see what was asked and who asked.
3. Pick the **Library folder** and **Quality**, or leave them on **Default** to use the [Request rules](settings.md#request-rules).
4. For a show or artist, untick any seasons or albums you do not want. Approving adds only what you ticked.
5. Add a note if you like, and press **Approve**.

HolyArrs adds the title to the Library. With **Search when approved** on, movies are searched at once and shows at the next feed check.

For a show already in the Library, approving turns on the asked seasons. For music, only the asked albums are kept up to date. An asked album of a kind the artist normally leaves out, such as a single or a live album, is turned on for that artist.

To clear a long list, use **Approve all**.

## Decline a request

1. Open the request and press **Decline**.
2. Write a short reason, or pick a quick reason. The people who asked see it.

You can undo for a few seconds after you decline.

## Who asked

The movie, show or artist page has a Requests card. It says who asked, for which seasons or albums, and who approved.

## Troubleshooting

**"Tick at least one to approve."** You unticked every season or album. Tick at least one, or decline the request.

**The approved title is not searched.** Check that **Search when approved** is on in the [Request rules](settings.md#request-rules). Shows wait for the next feed check.
