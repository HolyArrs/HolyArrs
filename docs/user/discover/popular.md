# New & Popular

Use New & Popular to see what just came out and what people are watching on the streaming services. Open it at `http://your-server:6262/popular`.

## Find something new

1. Open **New & Popular** from the menu.
2. Pick **All**, **TV shows**, **Movies** or **Music** at the top. **Music** only shows when the Music part is on.
3. Scroll the rows. Press a card to open the title, then ask for it or add it.

The big title at the top changes each visit. It says why it is there, such as a new season or its place on a service's chart.

## Settings on this page

- **Service chips.** Some rows have a chip for each streaming service. Pick one to see only that service, or **All**. The pick is for that row.
- **Type.** If Music is turned off while you have it picked, the page goes back to **All**.

## Troubleshooting

- **New & Popular is getting ready.** The rows are still being built from outside lists. They fill in within a few minutes. Press **Explore** to browse in the meantime.
- **A row is missing.** The list behind it could not be fetched. It comes back on a later visit.

For rows picked for you, see [Explore](explore.md).
