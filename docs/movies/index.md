HolyArrs searches your Sources for each movie you want, sends the best release to a Downloader and files the result in your Library.

You add a movie once. After that the search repeats by itself until the movie has a copy that is good enough.

## Pages

- [Search for movies](search.md): how a release is found and picked, and how to choose one yourself.
- [Movie downloads](downloads.md): follow the queue, fix stuck downloads and settle movies in Review.
- [Organise movies](organise.md): where files go and how they are named.
- [Editions and collections](editions-collections.md): keep more than one cut of a film and group movies in a series.

If this is your first movie, start with [First title](../start/first-title.md).
