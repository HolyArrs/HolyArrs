# Commercial licence

HolyArrs is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0. For almost everyone, that is all you need, and it costs nothing.

## You don't need a commercial licence if

- you run HolyArrs at home, for yourself, family or friends
- you run an unchanged copy, for anyone, even at work
- you change the code and, when you share it or let others use it over a network, you offer them your changed source under the AGPL-3.0

The AGPL already lets you use, change, share and even sell HolyArrs, as long as you follow its terms. The main one: if people use your changed version over a network, or you hand them a copy, they must be able to get its source under the AGPL.

## You may want a commercial licence if

you want to do any of these **without** following the AGPL's terms:

- ship HolyArrs inside a closed product you sell, like a NAS, a device or an app
- sell it under your own name or as a hosted service, and keep your changes private
- run a changed version for other people and not share your changes

A commercial licence is a separate written agreement with Luis (CoderLuii). It replaces the AGPL's sharing duty for the uses it lists. It does not give you the right to use the HolyArrs name or logo unless it says so.
